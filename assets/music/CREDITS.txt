Emberforge original procedural soundtrack

Twelve original instrumental loops composed and synthesized for this project.
No third-party samples, recordings, or existing melodies were used.
Source score and synthesizer: tools/generate-music.cjs in the private source repository.
Format: mono PCM WAV, 22,050 Hz, 16-bit. Circular note tails and room reflections.

forest - 이끼숲의 숨결: modal flute and soft wind
mine - 물에 잠긴 갱도: spacious bells, low drone, water drops
depths - 심해의 맥박: darker intervals and a low diving pulse
tower - 수문장의 회랑: resonant bells and measured footsteps
altar - 별빛의 순환: slow luminous bells and sustained fifths
battle - 엄니와 칼날: quick plucked ostinato and hand drums
trial - 마지막 한 단: tense semitone motif and steady percussion
rain - 비에 젖은 손님: quiet plucked melody and rain texture
home - 두 사람의 망치: warm major pentatonic melody
triumph - 돌아오는 길: bright bells and a gentle celebratory pulse
